# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Designers and art directors assembling a small set of mixed media into an editorial collage. They need to upload, order, explore, and quickly compare compositions without manually placing every card.

## Product Purpose

Collage Lab turns a list of local media into an interactive composition. Its success is a clear, expressive starting point that remains controllable through a small set of meaningful settings.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, editorial, tactile.

## Anti-references

Avoid dashboard-like decoration, rigid photo grids when the composition should feel authored, and visual effects that obscure the media.

## Design Principles

- Media carries the visual character; controls remain calm and compact.
- Each setting must produce a legible compositional change.
- Direct exploration should feel physical and predictable.
- Preserve a repeatable result while leaving room for discovery.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keep controls keyboard reachable with visible focus. Respect reduced motion and ensure that interaction is not communicated only through hover.
